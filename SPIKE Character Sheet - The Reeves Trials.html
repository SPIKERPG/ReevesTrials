■ THE WORLD YOU ARE ENTERING
⚠ CURRENT THREAT CONDITION // SPIKE STORM EVENT

Approximately one week before this briefing, a SPIKE Storm of unprecedented scale swept the global population. Casualties confirmed. Origin unknown. This event — the first of its kind in five years of post-Reset peace — is the reason you are standing in this briefing room. The UGD has expedited this commission. Treat elevated SPIKE readings in the field as a priority concern.

WORLD SUMMARY // YEAR 5 POST-RESET

World War III ended not with a treaty but with a silence. After years of cascading collapse — war, famine, pandemic, societal breakdown — a global neurological intervention known as the Reset stabilized the surviving population's emotional baseline. SPIKE ended. Rebuilding began.

You live in a world that works. The UGD administers global governance. Nations no longer exist — only districts. Communications have just been restored. Basic needs are met. It is stable, functional, and at peace. You are grateful for this. Everyone is. The Reset is not questioned. It is the foundation on which everything rests.

Your character knows they feel things — mild preferences, mild discomforts, purpose, care. What they don't know is what it felt like before. They have no reference point for what they are missing. Neither does anyone else.

STANDARD OPERATIVE EQUIPMENT
ISSUED AT UGD HQ
UGD UNIFORM SUBCUTANEOUS SPIKE IMPLANT FLASHLIGHT FIELD RADIO FIELD LOADOUT (SEE REQUISITION)

The subcutaneous SPIKE implant reads your own SPIKE level continuously and can evaluate others at close range — replacing the need for a handheld wand in the field. Wands remain in civilian use. Record your implant as part of your standard kit; no stat entry required.

WHAT YOUR CHARACTER FEELS
  • Mild satisfaction — a job done well, a problem solved
  • Mild discomfort — something that doesn't sit right
  • Preference — some things more than others
  • Care — about teammates, practically expressed
  • Curiosity — intellectual, not emotional
  • Purpose — they believe in the mission

Deep grief, passionate love, fear as physical sensation, rage, wonder — these are absent at baseline. SPIKE is what brings them back.

■ WHO IS YOUR CHARACTER
PLAYING AS YOURSELF (ENCOURAGED)

You are welcome — even encouraged — to play a version of yourself, post-Reset. This is not required, but it is a powerful entry point into the game's central theme. If your character is essentially you, the emotional flatness becomes immediately personal and legible. You know exactly what you'd remember. You know exactly what you can no longer feel.

  • You keep your real skills and knowledge. Your actual job, hobbies, and areas of expertise map directly to skill selection. A doctor takes Medicine. A programmer takes Computing. A soldier takes Firearms and Mobility.
  • Your emotional connections still exist as facts, not feelings. You know you loved someone. You have records proving it. You cannot locate the feeling of it.
  • Your backstory is real but experienced as though it belongs to someone else — like reading an account of your own life in a file.
ORIGIN // FROM ANYWHERE IN THE WORLD

Your operative can originate from any region of the former world. Nations no longer exist — only UGD administrative districts. But geography, language, and local habit survived the Reset intact. Cultural identity persists as a set of facts and reflexes, even without the felt connection to them.

  • Language still exists. Local customs persist as habit rather than felt tradition.
  • Food preferences remain. You still make your coffee a certain way without knowing why.
  • Regional expertise is valid: if you grew up near the sea, you have familiarity with coastal environments. If you grew up in a city, you navigate urban space differently than a rural operative.
  • You arrived at UGD HQ in Copenhagen via whatever means are available in Year 5. The UGD arranges travel for commissioned operatives.
■ BUILDING A BACKSTORY THAT FITS THE WORLD
THE CORE CHALLENGE

Your character has a past that was emotionally full. They lost people. They loved people. They did things they would have been proud of, or ashamed of, or both. All of that is still in them as information. None of it aches. The Reset converted autobiographical memory into semantic memory — facts without connection. Trauma, passion, deep love: known but not felt.

The craft challenge is writing a backstory that was vivid before the Reset and is now experienced as reading about a stranger who happened to share your name and face.

TIP // WRITE IN PAST TENSE, THIRD PERSON
Draft your backstory as if describing someone else's life you've read about. "She was a field medic during the Collapse. She lost her unit outside Warsaw. She kept working anyway." Then put yourself in that person's shoes — with all the facts and none of the weight.
TIP // INCLUDE ONE PERSON YOU LOVED
You know this fact. You have evidence — photographs, journal entries, records. You can describe them accurately. You cannot locate what it felt like to see them walk into a room. That gap is the dramatic material.
TIP // INCLUDE ONE THING YOU LOST
A home. A livelihood. A future you had planned. You know this happened. You know, factually, that it should feel like something. It registers instead as a note in a file: significant loss, pre-Reset period.
TIP // INCLUDE A SURVIVING SKILL OR HABIT
Muscle memory survived the Reset intact. Professional training survived. If you were a cook, you still cook well, automatically, without pleasure. If you were a runner, you still run — it's just exercise now, not release. One deeply ingrained skill or habit that runs on autopilot.
TIP // INCLUDE ONE THING YOU AVOID
Something you steer around without knowing why. A particular piece of music. A specific kind of weather. A word. You can't explain the avoidance. You've learned not to examine it too closely. This is a ghost of something the Reset couldn't fully reach. It will matter when your SPIKE rises.
TIP // BACKSTORY SHARING IS OPTIONAL
Your character has no particular reason to share their history with strangers. In a Reset world, personal history is exchanged like logistical information — when it's relevant, not as bonding. Let your backstory emerge in play, slowly, through what you avoid and what you reach for.

Concealable, fast, mobile. Light weapons deal damage equal to the margin of success only — no modifier. Best suited for operatives who value speed and concealment over stopping power.

ARMOR SELECT ONE — RECORD ON PAGE 1
LIGHT ARMOR  //  −2 DAMAGE RECEIVED

Soft-plate concealed armor. Reduces all incoming damage by 2. No penalties to movement or skill checks.

MEDIUM ARMOR  //  −4 DAMAGE RECEIVED

Tactical hard-plate carrier. Reduces all incoming damage by 4. Imposes −1 to Mobility checks due to encumbrance.

HEAVY ARMOR  //  −6 DAMAGE RECEIVED

Full tactical rig. Reduces all incoming damage by 6. Imposes −1 to Mobility checks and −1 to AWARENESS checks. Extended movement costs +1 COMPOSURE instead of the standard rule.

FIELD EQUIPMENT SELECT UP TO 3 ADDITIONAL ITEMS — RECORD ON PAGE 1
MEDKIT

Full trauma kit. On use: roll d10 + BODY — the result is the number of HP restored to the target. Requires an Action. Single use per kit.

BANDAGES

Basic wound dressing. Stabilises a downed operative (brings them to 1 HP). No roll required. Requires a Minor Action. Does not restore HP beyond stabilisation.

HACKING KIT

Portable intrusion hardware. Required for Computing checks against secured electronic systems, terminals, and locked doors. Without a kit, such checks cannot be attempted.

FLASHBANG

Disorientation grenade. On use: all targets in CLOSE range must make a d10 + AWARENESS vs TN 11 check or suffer Disadvantage on all rolls until the end of their next turn. Requires an Action. Single use.

BREACH CHARGE

Shaped explosive for forced entry. Destroys a standard door, wall section, or lock. Requires an Action to place and a Minor Action to detonate. Can be set on a delay. Single use.

HANDCUFFS

Restraint device. A restrained target cannot take physical actions. Applying requires a successful Close Combat check vs TN 9 on an incapacitated or willing target.

SPIKE WAND

Civilian-grade SPIKE diagnostic device. Reads the SPIKE level of a target at CLOSE or NEAR range. Requires a Minor Action. All UGD operatives carry a subcutaneous implant that reads their own level continuously — this wand is for evaluating others in the field when your implant's close-range scan is insufficient.
